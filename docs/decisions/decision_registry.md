explicitly open decisions. D001–D020 originated under V3 and remain in the
historical record unless amended below. The three synchronized V4 roadmap
documents dated 2026-08-26 are authoritative for the current protocol; D021–
D025 record the V4 architecture amendment, D026 records its pre-Week-3
Chain-feature availability hardening, and E006 records the completed Week 3B.0
feature-level evidence outcome without replacing E005.

## Change-control protocol

1. A proposed change to any `LOCKED` decision must include a written rationale and identify the initiating evidence or constraint.
2. Update this decision registry first; do not silently change a config, code path, report, dashboard, thesis, or slide.
3. Before approval, assess impact on all three authoritative V4 roadmaps,
   configs, data contracts, code, tests, generated artifacts, reports,
   dashboard, thesis, and reproducibility manifests. Preserve V3 as history.
4. If the change affects a core decision, update all synchronized authoritative documents before continuing the affected experiment.
5. Preserve an auditable record of the old decision, new decision, rationale, approver/date, and artifacts requiring regeneration.
6. Never change a decision, threshold, feature, model, pairing rule, simulation parameter, objective, solver protocol, or robustness setting because of observed 2024 results.
7. `TBD` does not mean permission to guess. Resolve a TBD only with the evidence specified in its change rule, then explicitly change its status and record the resolution.
